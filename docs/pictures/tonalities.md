# Tonalities

A tonality is five colours for dark mode and five for light: the base the picture sits on, a deep tone, two accents
and a shade. Every space shows the same tonality, even with [a picture per space](../desktop/spaces.md). All of these
are the same *waves* picture; only the tonality changes.

| Tonality | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember**<br>Amber and violet on near-black; cream and lilac in light mode. | ![ember, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/ember-dark.jpg) | ![ember, light](../images/shots/tonalities/ember-light.jpg) |
| **Tide**<br>Ocean blues and cyan. | ![tide, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/tide-dark.jpg) | ![tide, light](../images/shots/tonalities/tide-light.jpg) |
| **Aurora**<br>Green and violet light. | ![aurora, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/aurora-dark.jpg) | ![aurora, light](../images/shots/tonalities/aurora-light.jpg) |
| **Dusk**<br>Rose, orange and plum. | ![dusk, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/dusk-dark.jpg) | ![dusk, light](../images/shots/tonalities/dusk-light.jpg) |
| **Moss**<br>Leaf green and gold. | ![moss, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/moss-dark.jpg) | ![moss, light](../images/shots/tonalities/moss-light.jpg) |
| **Glacier**<br>Cold greys and ice blue. | ![glacier, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/glacier-dark.jpg) | ![glacier, light](../images/shots/tonalities/glacier-light.jpg) |
| **Citrus**<br>Orange and yellow on warm brown. | ![citrus, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/citrus-dark.jpg) | ![citrus, light](../images/shots/tonalities/citrus-light.jpg) |
| **Mono**<br>Greys only. | ![mono, dark](../images/shots/tonalities/mono-dark.jpg) | ![mono, light](../images/shots/tonalities/mono-light.jpg) |

Lock one under **Tonality** in the menu, or leave it on **Random**: a random tonality changes on every shuffle,
independently of the style.

# Styles

Each style below is shown in one tonality, dark on the left and light on the right; every style works in every
[tonality](tonalities.md). Lock one under **Style** in the menu, or leave it on **Random**.

## Mesh

Soft glows over a dark base. The quietest style, and the one closest to a plain colour.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![mesh in ember, dark](../images/shots/styles/mesh-dark.jpg) | ![mesh in ember, light](../images/shots/styles/mesh-light.jpg) |

## Flow

Marbled, slowly folding colour: noise bent by more noise, run through the tonality.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![flow in dusk, dark](../images/shots/styles/flow-dark.jpg) | ![flow in dusk, light](../images/shots/styles/flow-light.jpg) |

## Waves

Layered ribbons, back to front, each with a lit rim and a shadow on the one behind.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![waves in tide, dark](../images/shots/styles/waves-dark.jpg) | ![waves in tide, light](../images/shots/styles/waves-light.jpg) |

## Orbs

Large spheres lit from the top left, glowing onto the glows behind them.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![orbs in aurora, dark](../images/shots/styles/orbs-dark.jpg) | ![orbs in aurora, light](../images/shots/styles/orbs-light.jpg) |

## Rings

Concentric bands round a centre below one of the bottom corners, each shaded like a tube.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![rings in citrus, dark](../images/shots/styles/rings-dark.jpg) | ![rings in citrus, light](../images/shots/styles/rings-light.jpg) |

## Dunes

Hills stacked towards you, each lit along its ridge, the sky behind them.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![dunes in dusk, dark](../images/shots/styles/dunes-dark.jpg) | ![dunes in dusk, light](../images/shots/styles/dunes-light.jpg) |

## Contour

Topographic lines, every fifth one stronger, about a point wide on any display.

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![contour in glacier, dark](../images/shots/styles/contour-dark.jpg) | ![contour in glacier, light](../images/shots/styles/contour-light.jpg) |

## Silk

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|
| ![silk in ember, dark](../images/shots/styles/silk-dark.jpg) | ![silk in ember, light](../images/shots/styles/silk-light.jpg) |

| Dark | Light |
|---|---|

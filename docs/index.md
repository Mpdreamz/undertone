# Undertone

Hide the notch. Keep the colour.

Undertone is a small macOS menu bar app that hides the notch and the screen's borders in black, and fills the rest
with backgrounds it draws itself: gradients and shapes in one of eight tonalities, the light half in light mode and the
dark half in dark mode, a new one every hour, day or wake if you like.
